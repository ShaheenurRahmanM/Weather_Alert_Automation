🌦️ Weather Alert Settings

Customize your daily weather notification & rain alerts. Everything here is 100% free and delivered as a push notification via ntfy.sh — no account or login needed.

📲 Step 1 — Set up the app

  1. Install the free ntfy app: Android · iPhone · or use the web app.
  2. Pick a secret topic name below (like a password — make it unique).
  3. In the ntfy app, tap + → Subscribe to topic → enter the exact same name.

🔔 Notification channel

Use something random & private. Anyone who knows this name can send you notifications.
